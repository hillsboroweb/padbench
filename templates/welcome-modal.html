Hey, welcome to PadBench!

Quick heads-up before we get hands-on:

  • We’re a fan-made tool, not connected to Sony or PlayStation.
  • Use your good judgment; controller calibration is your call.
  • PadBench does not send analytics or controller readings to a server.
  • Keep the battery connected and charged. Losing power during a permanent save can damage the controller.
  • Try a temporary calibration first. Test it before saving anything permanently.
