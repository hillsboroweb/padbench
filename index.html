       This tool can’t fix worn-out stick parts.
    If the stick is worn, dirty, or damaged, calibration won’t repair it. Replacing the part is the real fix. Once you’ve swapped in a new stick, this tool can help calibrate it.
       Trying to calibrate a worn-out stick can make things worse, so check it out before saving anything.
